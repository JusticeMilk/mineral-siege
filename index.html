How to play
  • Seal the gap in the canyon wall first. Your worker never leaves the pocket.
  • Wall, Turret and Drill each open a picker. Walls come with traps (Spikes, Stasis fields that slow bugs, and Mines), which can go a few tiles outside your wall.
  • Turrets: Gun for steady fire, Cannon for splash, Flamer for close-range burning, Laser for piercing shots. Turrets inside a Power Core's field hit harder, and turrets next to a different turret type get a link bonus.
  • Every building gets a new look every 5 upgrade levels. Upgrade your Hub too: level 2 adds auto-repair, level 3 adds a hub cannon.
  • Build one Research Lab and tap it for boosts that last the whole run.
  • Abilities on the right of the map: Orbital Strike (tap it, then tap the target), Shield, and Ore Surge.
  • Every 5th night brings a boss, rotating through four kinds. Every 10th night is a siege from all sides. Glowing rings mark elite bugs with special traits.
  • Every level gives an upgrade point. Reach level 20 to prestige for a lasting ore and XP bonus.
