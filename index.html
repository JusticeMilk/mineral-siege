How to play
  • Seal the gap in the canyon wall first. Your worker never leaves the pocket.
  • Drill Rigs make ore on their own. Upgrading one makes it pump faster and pay out more.
  • Turrets go behind the wall. Every building can be upgraded many times. With Upgrade selected, the next cost floats over each building.
  • Healers repair nearby buildings, but only inside a Power Core's field. Upgrading a Power Core widens the field and boosts its healers.
  • Every 5th night is a boss night with Behemoths and a bigger swarm. Purple creep spreads closer every night. Drag the map or tap the minimap to look around.
  • Every level gives an upgrade point. Reach level 20 to prestige for a lasting ore and XP bonus.
